Petals Soft and Free

Petals soft like morning dew,
Painted in each vibrant hue,
Crimson, gold, and violet deep,
Dancing while the world's asleep.

Sunflowers tall, they turn to greet,
The golden rays so warm and sweet,
While roses blush in gardens fair,
Spreading fragrance through the air.

Daisies white with centers bright,
Tulips swaying left and right,
Lilies pure as fallen snow,
In the gentle wind they blow.

From the bud to full display,
Flowers brighten up the day,
Nature's masterpiece so true,
A gift of grace in every bloom they grew.
