Golden Light

Waking softly through the morning mist,
A golden warmth upon my face,
The sun ascends with graceful twist,
And bathes the world in light's embrace.

Rays of brilliance pierce the day,
Dancing on the dewy grass,
Painting shadows where they play,
As golden hours gently pass.

Clouds drift by in silver white,
Catching fire at day's own peak,
Sunshine spills its warmth so bright,
On every cheek that it does seek.

Bringing life to all below,
Flowers lift their faces high,
Fed by that resplendent glow,
Beneath the vast and endless sky.

When evening comes and sun retreats,
Its gentle warmth lingers still,
In memories that sunshine keeps,
On hearts it blessed upon the hill.

For in that golden, glowing light,
We find our peace, our hope, our way,
The sun reminds us day and night,
That joy returns with every day.
